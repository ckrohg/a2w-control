# so the daily check goes GREEN on the accepted baseline and RED only on NEW drift. Every ack must
# carry an expiry: on that date it turns red again by itself and has to be consciously renewed or
# fixed. That is the whole point — an ack is a dated decision, never a permanent silence.

# was known, tracked (#117) and deliberately deferred. A permanently-red check is worse than none:

# outage email that was delivered and never opened.
#

# narrow. Do NOT ack "unrecognised phase_b result" or "hub unreachable": those mean the instrument
# broke, not that a known condition persists.
