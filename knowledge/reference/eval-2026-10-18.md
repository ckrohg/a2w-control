## 3 · Findings

**F1 — The Pi drops off the hub for 2–3 h about once a day; nothing is designed for that shape.** Phase B `no Pi
connected` failures cluster in five blocks: Oct 14 15–17Z, Oct 15 18–20Z, Oct 16 10–12Z, Oct 17 05:31–07:41Z (hub
timeline: one disconnect, one reconnect, 2 h 10 min apart), Oct 17 15–17Z. Not at a fixed hour, so not a cron; too long
for a WiFi flap (the hub also sees 5–21 short flaps/day, a separate symptom); the bridge-watchdog reboot ladder (#85)
would reconnect in minutes, not hours. Cause needs the Pi's own journal (uptime, wlan0, bridge restarts) — the
`system_stats.uptime_s` series in the mirror DB would say whether it reboots. During each window: no setpoint tracking,
no probe can start, the lease (if it were armed) would lapse and revert — correct — but see F2. Reopen #76 with this
table; it is the single biggest Safe-tier item and it needs the house network or Tailscale.

**F2 — During a Pi dropout the HBX writer still writes, against stale pump state, and it violated I1.** The hub keeps
serving the Pi's last `pumps[]` with `pi_connected:false`; `writes.ts` checks I1 against `state.pumps` only (filters on
`p.online`, never reads `pi_connected` or `ts`). At 17:51Z Oct 17, inside the 15–17Z dropout, the auto-pilot set 145 °F;
Phase B could not lead; at 17:55Z the monitor logged `I1 VIOLATION: pump1 setpoint 140.0°F < required 147.0°F`. The
rehearsal's `pi-offline` scenario makes the hub return 503, which the writer does refuse — so the harness proves the
wrong failure shape. Fix: refuse (or hold) HBX writes when `pi_connected` is false or `ts` is older than ~10 min, and add
a `pi-dropped-hub-up` scenario. Small PR, deploys the planner.

**F3 — The soak's identity is lost in the winter-guard branch (the #155 class, second instance).** `shadow.ts` keeps the
`sani` flag but rewrites the reason to `winter guard: mimic HBX curve …` when the as-found curve outruns the target
below `winterGuardF`. Observed twice (Oct 17 08:40Z and 17:51Z): `set 145°F — winter guard`, i.e. a soak hour raised to
the as-found curve under a degraded-mode reason. The auto-pilot caps correctly by flag (145 allowed), but the TempIQ
poster files the kind from the reason and the identification driver's exclusion matches `/sanitize/` in the reason — so
it is posted as `autopilot`, and the driver may not keep its distance. Same fix shape as #155, one branch over.

**F4 — OutageWatch has been down since 2026-03-15.** Railway shows the last deployment CRASHED on a `PostgresError:
unable to check out connection from the pool`; `/api/status` answers 502. The planner treats unreachable as "no signal",
so nothing false-armed — but the storm feature's grid-outage input has not existed this year and no document says so.
Decide: fix the service (likely a dead or quota-limited database) or remove the dependency and say so in `/health`.

**F5 — The drift-check is red with no one listening.** Red every scheduled run since Oct 11 because the #117 ack
expired; the failure step emails only if `RESEND_API_KEY`/`RESEND_TO` are repo secrets, and they are not (the three
secrets are `BACKUP_PASSPHRASE`, `DATABASE_URL`, `HUB_CLIENT_TOKEN`). The check that was built to make FINDING-1-class
rot loud is currently silent. Two secrets and one ack line fix it.

**F6 — Three unverified zones bind the floor and have no escalation headroom.** Tonight's `[demand]` warnings named
Upstairs Bathroom (floor 99.5–103.4 °F) and Kitchen Radiant (106.3 °F) as binding and UNVERIFIED. #134 shows that for
these zones the cost-first escalation collapses to zero degrees at any room deficit. The fix is five minutes in TempIQ
(confirm delivery types) and has been on the owner list since September.

**F7 — TempIQ's heating-readiness check errored on its first heating-weather run.** `heating_weather=1 … notified=0
errors=1`. This is the reminder that a Nest left in cool/off makes a room invisible to the demand floor. Needs a look on
the TempIQ side before it matters (first forecast low ≤ 45 °F already happened).

**F8 — The kanban no longer describes the system.** Of the 45 open a2w cards, at least 20 are shipped (W1-UI #23–30
merged 07-15; #36 single-writer and the lease now ON in prod; #58, #78, #94, #112, #114, #116, #118, #119, #133b, #135,
#136, #137, #121 asserted). `tenet doctor` reports 0/28 milestoned. A new session cannot tell what is open from the board.

**F9 — Pump 2 sat idle at 16 °C inlet while pump 1 heated at 32 °F outdoor.** Not a fault (0 active), the HBX simply did
not call stage 2 while the tank recovered at ~8 °F/h. Worth one glance on the first night the tank does not recover:
HP2's known pattern is winter failure-to-start (E21 history).

**F10 — Housekeeping with winter consequences.** (a) `railway.toml` is deprecated 2026-12-01 and carries the
`watchPatterns` (#164 filed). (b) `/api/hbx/target` still reports `curve_in_force.shaped` with the old `dbt−mbt > 4`
predicate (false) while `/health` uses the new one (true) — the dashboard reads the former. (c) The backup has never been
restored; the passphrase lives only in GitHub and the owner's Keychain on another Mac.
